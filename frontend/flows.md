# Frontend flows

- **Feed.** Global feed for everyone. Personal feed when signed in. Tag filter is a third pill.
- **Article.** Banner with author meta, body, tags, comments. Authors see edit and delete. Other signed-in users see follow and favorite.
- **Editor.** Create or update an article.
- **Follow.** Toggle follow on article and profile surfaces.
- **Favorite.** Heart control on feed cards, profile article lists, and (for non-authors) the article page. Product policy for an author favoriting their own article is in [product/favorites.md](../product/favorites.md).
- **Settings.** Update profile and password for the signed-in user.
- **Profile.** Articles the user wrote, or articles they favorited.
