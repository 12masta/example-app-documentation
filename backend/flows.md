# Backend flows

- List and get articles (global, feed, tag, author, favorited)
- Create, update, and delete articles
- Favorite and unfavorite an article
- Follow and unfollow a profile
- Comments on an article
- Current user and settings

Favorite persistence is a backend operation. Whether the SPA should *send* that operation for an author on their own article is product policy in [product/favorites.md](../product/favorites.md).
